Best AI Resume Builder 2026: 8 Tools Compared

Updated 2026-09-05 · 10 min read · Applio
The best AI resume builder depends on what you need. For a genuinely free plan with AI writing and built-in ATS scoring, use Applio. For the tightest ATS-focused resume builder, use Rezi. For a full job-search pipeline with a resume builder attached, use Teal. For visually striking templates, use Enhancv or Kickresume. For traditional design-first builders, use Resume.io or Zety. Full ranked comparison below.
Editorial disclosure: Applio publishes this comparison and is included in the ranking. We attempt to describe every competitor accurately based on public pricing and features as of September 2026. This page has no affiliate links, no sponsored placements, and no paid rankings.
Rankings

  2. Rezi — best pure ATS optimization

How we ranked them

Every AI resume builder claims the same things: ATS-friendly templates, AI writing, one-click formatting. The differences show up in the details. We ranked these 8 tools on five criteria:

  • Free plan usefulness: Can you actually build and download a resume without paying?
  • ATS scoring: Does the tool score your resume against a specific job description in real time?
  • AI writing quality: Is the AI grounded in your actual experience, or does it generate generic filler?
  • Template quality: Do the templates parse cleanly on Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever, not just look nice?
  • Value at typical pricing: How much do you pay per month for what you get?

Rankings assume you're a job seeker building or improving one main resume, not a career coach managing 20 client accounts.

The 8 best AI resume builders in 2026

#1

Applio

Best genuinely-free plan with built-in ATS scoring

Applio is a free AI resume builder that combines a real free plan (with 10 PDF downloads per month), a built-in 0-100 ATS score against any job description, and AI writing grounded in your real experience. The free plan gives you all 25 templates, a job tracker, and AI interview prep. Premium ($9.99/month) or lifetime ($39.99 one-time) unlocks unlimited downloads and the full AI writing suite.

Strengths

  • Only builder here where the free plan lets you download without a subscription trap
  • Built-in ATS score with keyword matching
  • 25 ATS-friendly templates (Harvard, Stanford, Wharton, MIT, FAANG, and more)
  • AI grounded in your real experience — won't fabricate
  • Includes cover letters, interview prep, job tracker, career coach

Drawbacks

  • Newer than most alternatives (2024)
  • Fewer visually experimental templates than Enhancv or Kickresume
  • Solo-founder built — no big brand presence yet
Free plan: Yes, real one Paid: $9.99/mo or $39.99 lifetime ATS score: Built in Templates: 25
Best for: Anyone who wants a full-featured AI resume builder without a subscription trap. Especially strong for students, career changers, and anyone building their first or second full resume.
#2

Rezi

Best pure ATS optimization

Rezi is the tightest ATS-focused resume builder in the market. Its core workflow is: paste a job description, get a live keyword-match score, iterate. The AI writing is tightly coupled to keyword suggestions from the job posting. Free plan is limited (1 resume, capped AI usage); Rezi Pro at $29/month or $99/year unlocks the full experience.

Strengths

  • Real-time ATS scoring with keyword suggestions
  • All templates are single-column and ATS-safe by default
  • Focus makes the product feel deliberate, not bloated
  • Annual pricing (~$8/month) is competitive

Drawbacks

  • Free plan is too limited to be useful
  • Job tracker is minimal compared to Teal or Applio
  • Fewer templates (~12) and less visual variety
  • Monthly plan is expensive at $29/month
Free plan: Limited Paid: $29/mo or $99/yr ATS score: Built in, real-time Templates: ~12
Best for: Applicants who care about ATS optimization above all else and are willing to pay for it. Not the best choice if the free plan matters or you want broader career tools.
#3

Teal

Best full job-search pipeline

Teal is a job-search operating system with a resume builder attached. Its Chrome extension lets you save any job from any site in one click, then tracks status, next steps, and networking outreach. The resume builder integrates with the job tracker so you can tailor a resume to a saved posting. Free plan covers the tracker and basic builder; Teal+ (~$29/month, ~$9/week) adds AI resume writing and ATS scoring.

Strengths

  • Best-in-class job tracker with Chrome extension
  • Contact/networking CRM built in
  • Weekly pricing option ($9/week) fits short job searches
  • Cover letter and email templates alongside resume

Drawbacks

  • Resume builder is a secondary feature, not the core
  • Templates are limited (~9) and less polished than dedicated builders
  • Monthly pricing is expensive (~$29/mo) with no annual discount
  • Feature bloat can feel overwhelming for basic resume needs
Free plan: Yes, useful Paid: $9/wk or $29/mo ATS score: Teal+ only Templates: ~9
Best for: Active job seekers applying to 10-50 roles who want a full pipeline in one tool. Overkill if you just need to write one strong resume.
#4

Kickresume

Best template variety with AI writing

Kickresume offers ~40 visually distinct templates spanning traditional to bold-creative, plus AI writing powered by GPT-4. Templates are grouped by industry and role. Free plan is limited; paid plans start at ~$5/month annually (up to ~$20/month for monthly).

Strengths

  • Largest template variety in this list
  • Solid AI writing quality
  • Cover letter and website builder included
  • Annual pricing is reasonable (~$5/mo)

Drawbacks

  • Many templates are multi-column and struggle with ATS parsing
  • No built-in ATS scoring
  • Free plan requires a Kickresume watermark on downloads
  • UI can feel cluttered
Free plan: Watermarked Paid: ~$5-20/mo ATS score: No Templates: ~40
Best for: People who want template variety above all else and are choosing a template primarily by visual appeal. Not the pick if ATS optimization is your priority.
#5

Enhancv

Best visually distinctive templates

Enhancv is known for the boldest, most visually experimental templates in the market — color blocks, icons, unique layouts. AI writing is included. Pricing sits at ~$25/month monthly or ~$16/month annually (Pro plan). The design-forward approach is love-or-hate for ATS.

Strengths

  • Most visually distinctive templates in the market
  • Great for creative, marketing, and design roles
  • AI writing quality is solid
  • Some templates are genuinely ATS-friendly (single column variants)

Drawbacks

  • Many templates use multi-column layouts that break ATS parsing
  • Expensive monthly pricing (~$25/mo)
  • Free plan doesn't allow PDF downloads without watermark
  • Icons and color blocks can distract from content
Free plan: Watermarked Paid: $16-25/mo ATS score: No Templates: ~30
Best for: Creative and design roles where a distinctive visual look matters and the resume goes primarily to humans, not ATS.
#6

Resume.io

Traditional design-first builder with subscription paywall

Resume.io is one of the largest resume builders by user base, with ~30 templates and a well-designed builder UI. AI writing added in 2024. Build-free, pay-to-download: $2.95 for a 7-day trial that auto-renews to ~$25/month. Same parent company as Zety (BOLD Ltd).

Strengths

  • Large template library (~30)
  • Polished UI, easy to use for first-time builders
  • Cover letter builder included
  • AI writing added in 2024

Drawbacks

  • Trial auto-renewal is aggressive — many complaints about charges
  • No ATS scoring
  • Cancel flow can be difficult (varies by country)
  • Many templates use multi-column layouts
Free plan: No downloads Paid: $2.95 trial → $25/mo ATS score: No Templates: ~30
Best for: Users who want a polished traditional builder and don't mind the subscription. See our full Zety vs Resume.io comparison for details on the parent company overlap.
#7

Zety

Near-clone of Resume.io with same subscription model

Zety and Resume.io share a parent company (BOLD Ltd.) and offer near-identical products. ~20 templates, AI writing added in 2024, build-free, pay-to-download at ~$24/month. Feature parity is close to 100%.

Strengths

  • Corporate/traditional template aesthetic
  • Good for finance, consulting, and business roles
  • Well-established brand and reviews
  • Annual pricing (~$71/yr) is competitive

Drawbacks

  • Trial auto-renewal complaints similar to Resume.io
  • No ATS scoring
  • Fewer templates than Resume.io
  • Effectively the same product as Resume.io
Free plan: No downloads Paid: $2.95 trial → $24/mo ATS score: No Templates: ~20
Best for: Same audience as Resume.io. Pick whichever design you prefer; there's no meaningful functional difference between the two.
#8

ChatGPT (as a resume tool)

Free text generator, not a full resume workflow

ChatGPT can generate resume bullet points, summaries, and cover letter drafts. But it doesn't format the document, provide templates, score your resume against ATS, or export a properly structured PDF. Most people using ChatGPT for resumes end up copy-pasting output into a separate builder anyway.

Strengths

  • Free (with GPT-3.5) or $20/month (Plus)
  • Flexible: can help with any writing task
  • Good for generating first drafts of bullets
  • Available in multiple languages

Drawbacks

  • No templates, formatting, or PDF export
  • No ATS scoring or keyword matching
  • Will fabricate content if you're not careful
  • You still need a separate tool to build the actual resume
Free plan: GPT-3.5 Paid: $20/mo (Plus) ATS score: No Templates: None
Best for: Quick text generation as a supplement to a real resume builder, not as a standalone solution. See how to use ChatGPT for your resume.
