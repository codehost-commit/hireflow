Why "Google Docs style" is the safest resume format

Every year, "resume template google docs" is one of the top three searched resume queries. The reason is simple: people know Google Docs looks clean, recruiters recognize the format instantly, and there's no visual gimmickry to distract from the content. It's the format that says "I'm a professional; here's my work."

Applio's Google Docs template captures that aesthetic: Arial body text, a restrained blue accent on section headings, single-column layout, and standard section names (Experience, Education, Skills). It parses cleanly on every major ATS because it doesn't contain anything unusual to parse.
