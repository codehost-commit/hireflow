Why a sales resume needs its own template

Sales hiring managers read resumes differently. They aren't scanning for narrative — they're pattern-matching for numbers: quota attainment, deal size, pipeline, sales cycle, win rate. If those numbers aren't visually easy to find within the first five seconds, the resume goes to the bottom of the pile.

The Sales template pulls those metrics forward. Each role has visual room for quota attainment percentage, average deal size, and pipeline generated as prominent callouts, not buried in body text. Awards (President's Club, Circle of Excellence) get a dedicated section. CRM and prospecting tools (Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Gong, ZoomInfo, Apollo) list in a skills section formatted for ATS keyword matching.

Every Applio template is ATS-friendly: single-column, standard headings, real text. The Sales template layers the sales-specific visual hierarchy on top.

Sales template, FAQ

What should a sales resume emphasize?

Quota attainment percentage, average deal size, pipeline generated, number of accounts closed, sales cycle length, and the specific CRM and prospecting tools you use.

Who should use the Sales resume template?

AEs, SDRs, BDRs, enterprise account managers, sales managers and directors, and any commissioned or quota-carrying role.

Should I list my President's Club or Rep of the Year awards?

Yes. Sales-specific awards are a strong signal to hiring managers. Include them in an Awards section or inline with the relevant role.
