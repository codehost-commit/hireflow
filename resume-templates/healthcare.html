Why healthcare resumes need a different layout

A healthcare resume has to answer credentialing questions before it answers experience questions. When a hospital HR or agency recruiter reviews your resume, the first thing they need to verify is that you hold an active license, that it covers the state or region they're hiring for, and that your certifications (BLS, ACLS, PALS, specialty boards) are current. If those aren't easy to find, your resume goes to the bottom of the pile regardless of how strong your experience is.

The Healthcare template puts licensure and certifications directly under your name and contact info, before your professional summary. Clinical experience gets its own structured section with facility type, unit, and patient population. Continuing education, memberships, and languages appear in dedicated sub-sections rather than being crammed into a generic "additional" block.

Every Applio template is ATS-friendly: standard section headings, single-column flow, real selectable text. The Healthcare template adds the sections that matter for clinical hiring on top of that foundation.
